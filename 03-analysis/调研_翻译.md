# 六级翻译成品级填空模板——看到中文就知道怎么拆、每个空填什么

这份东西和杰哥认可的作文模板是同一种东西：整篇能背、能默写、拿到题只换关键词。作文是"开头2句+主体4-6句+结尾2句"，翻译也一样，只是骨架更短、挖的空更多是"专有名词 + 数字 + 动词"。

先把数据交代清楚，不吹牛：12段中文原文逐句读完了，其中**10段有参考译文可以逐句倒推**（2024.12三套、2025.12三套、2026.06三套，共9段"学姐制作"解析里的参考译文，外加2025.06第3套南水北调那段带双语逐句精析的）；**2025.06第1套天宫、第2套大湾区只有中文原文**——那两期解析是扫描件，PDF用pymupdf抽出来是0字符，没有官方参考译文。这两段我不装作有，第四部分用骨架现场套写示范，标清楚是"套写"不是"官方译文"。所有空都用 `［］` 标出来，照着填就能成文。

## 第一部分　成就类：把9段参考译文拆成同一篇骨架

### 1.1 先说破一件事：这9段其实是同一篇文章

把北斗、洋山港、神舟、南水北调、远程医疗、电商助农、无人机这7段有译文的成就类摆在一起逐句看，你会发现它们看着 topic 不同，**句子功能顺序几乎一模一样**。参考译文从来不是"想到哪写到哪"，而是下面这条流水线：

```
第1步 定位：这个东西是什么 / 取得了什么地位
第2步 背景：从什么时候起 / 经过多少年 / 随着什么趋势
第3步 过程：做了什么努力 / 攻克了什么 / 实现了什么
第4步 铺开：用在哪里 / 涉及哪些领域（such as 列举）
第5步 数据：多长 / 多少亿 / 多少个项目（数字句单独成句或挂在一个 with 结构里）
第6步 结果：使中国成为……之一 / 为……做出贡献 / making... 结果状语
第7步 收尾：如今得到广泛认可 / 未来将走得更远
```

这就是成就类的"成文骨架"。短版取1+3+4+6，中版取1+2+3+4+6+7，长版全取。**阅卷老师看到的不是你背没背原文，而是这7步在不在、每一步英语句子对不对。**

下面三个版本，每个空都标了"对应中文的什么内容、填什么"。把 `［］` 换成真题里的词，整段译文直接出来。

### 1.2 短版骨架（4句，约90-100词，保命用）

适合时间紧、字数不够但求稳。四句话刚好是"是什么—怎么做—用在哪—带来什么结果"。

```
[1] The ［成功研制/蓬勃发展］ of ［专有名词］ is a major
    ［achievement/development］ China has made since ［时间起点］.

    对应中文：第一句"……的成功研制是……的一项重大科技成就"或"近年来……蓬勃发展"。
    空里填：成功研制 = successful development；蓬勃发展 = booming development / has witnessed remarkable growth；
    专有名词 = 括号里给的英文直接抄（Beidou / Yangshan Port / drone）；
    时间起点 = "改革开放以来" → the reform and opening-up；"近年来" → 用长版，短版这里可写 in recent years。

[2] Through ［unremitting efforts / years of development］,
    ［研发人员/系统名称］ ［overcame 难题 / achieved 目标］, making China
    one of the few countries in the world to ［独立拥有……］.

    对应中文："经过不懈努力，攻克了一系列技术难题，最终实现了……，使中国成为世界上少数几个……国家之一"。
    空里填：不懈努力 = unremitting efforts；经过近20年发展 = after nearly 20 years of development；
    攻克难题 = overcame a series of technical difficulties；
    实现全球覆盖和高精度定位 = achieved global coverage and high-precision positioning；
    独立拥有全球卫星导航系统 = independently possess a global satellite navigation system。
    （注意：making 后面那截是结果状语，"使中国成为……"就往这里塞。）

[3] ［专有名词］ has been widely applied in many fields such as
    ［领域1］, ［领域2］, ［领域3］, and ［领域4］.

    对应中文："……已广泛应用于……、……、……等诸多领域"或"在民用领域得到广泛应用"。
    空里填：领域直接抄中文名词的英文——交通运输 transportation、灾害救援 traffic rescue、
    天气预报 weather forecasting、公共安全 public safety、民用领域 the civilian sector。
    中文只给1-2个领域时，把 such as 后面的并列项减到2个，别硬凑。

[4] ［专有名词］ has also gained wide recognition and begun to
    provide ［quality services / new momentum］ to ［对象］.

    对应中文："现在已经在国际上得到广泛认可，开始为越来越多的国家和地区提供优质服务"或
    "为经济社会发展注入新动能"。
    空里填：得到广泛认可 = gained wide international recognition；
    为越来越多的国家和地区 = to an increasing number of countries and regions；
    注入新动能 = injecting new momentum into economic and social development（用长版 making 结构更顺）。
```

四句连起来背，考试时第一步先把这四句默到草稿纸，再往里填词。**先有骨架，后填内容，就不会慌。**

### 1.3 中版骨架（6句，约110-125词，最推荐）

在短版基础上加"背景句"和"收尾句"，刚好卡在参考译文最常见的110-130词区间。

```
[1] ［主题引入句］.

    对应中文：第一段开头那句总起。三种写法任选其一：
    A. The successful development of ［X］ is a major technological achievement China has made since ［时间］.（北斗式）
    B. ［X］ is an important component of ［上位系统］, China's first ［类别］, and one of the largest ［类别］ in the world.（洋山港式）
    C. In recent years, China's ［行业］ industry has witnessed remarkable growth, with ［伴随数据］.（无人机式）
    空里填：把中文括号里的英文照抄进 ［X］，类别词从"重要组成部分/第一个/最大之一"里挑。

[2] Since/After/With ［时间或趋势］, ［主体］ has ［持续加大投入 / 进入快车道 / 实现高度自动化］.

    对应中文："经过近20年的发展……已实现高度自动化""改革开放以来……持续加大科研投入"
    "近年来，中国航天进入创新发展'快车道'"。
    空里填：时间起点 Since the reform and opening-up / After nearly 20 years of development /
    In recent years；"持续加大科研投入" = continuously increased investment in scientific research；
    "实现高度自动化" = achieved a high degree of automation；"进入快车道" = entered a "fast track" of innovative development。

[3] Through ［unremitting efforts / the use of 技术］, ［主体］
    ［overcame 难题 / reduced 成本 / enabled 操作］, achieving ［成果1］ and ［成果2］.

    对应中文："经过不懈努力，攻克了一系列技术难题""数字技术和人工智能的使用大大减少了用工成本和碳排放"
    "自主研发的码头管理系统可以远程操控"。
    空里填：unremitting efforts / the use of digital technology and artificial intelligence；
    reduced labor costs and carbon emissions / enabled remote control of large equipment from over a hundred kilometers away。
    技巧：中文"大大减少了A和B"→ has significantly reduced A and B，别直译成 greatly decreased。

[4] ［主体］ has been widely applied in many fields such as ［A］,
    ［B］, ［C］, and ［D］.

    对应中文：应用/覆盖句。填法和短版[3]完全一样，中版这里可多列1-2个领域。
    电网/支付/5G这类"列举成果"的句子，改成并列主语：The nationwide coverage of 5G networks,
    the rapid development of telemedicine, and the Chang'e lunar exploration program all demonstrate...
    （这是2025.12-2的原文写法，三个名词短语并列，一人一个逗号。）

[5] ［主体］ has now gained wide international recognition / has made
    significant contributions to ［人类文明进步/区域发展］, making China
    one of the few countries in the world to ［独特能力］.

    对应中文："在国际上得到广泛认可""为人类文明进步做出了巨大贡献""使中国成为世界上少数几个……之一"。
    空里填：wide international recognition / significant contributions to the progress of human civilization；
    "使……成为"一律用现在分词 making 收尾，这是成就类的招牌句型。

[6] In the future, ［主体］ will ［迈得更稳更远 / 继续发展并做出贡献］.

    对应中文："未来，中国探索太空的脚步将迈得更稳、更远""洋山港将不断发展……做出更大贡献"。
    空里填：take more solid and farther steps in its space exploration endeavors；
    continue to develop and make greater contributions to building ［城市］ into a ［定位］。
    没有未来句就不要硬加，把[6]删掉，靠[5]收尾一样完整。
```

背的时候按 1→2→3→4→5→6 的顺序整段默写，每个空只换三类东西：**专有名词、时间、动宾短语**。

### 1.4 长版骨架（8句，约130-145词，冲高分用）

中版再加"数据句"和"并列展开句"。数据句是8/12段中文都有的必考点，宁可少写一个修饰语，也要把数字写完整。

```
[1] ［定位句，同中版[1]任选A/B/C］.

[2] ［背景句，同中版[2]］.

[3] ［过程句，同中版[3]］.

[4] ［展开句：中文出现"不仅……还/也……"时用这个］
    ［主体］ not only will ［建成A和B］, but also ［打造成C］.

    对应中文："大湾区不仅将建成充满活力的世界级城市群和具有全球影响力的国际科技创新中心，
    还将打造成适合工作、购物、旅游的优质生活圈"。
    空里填：build a dynamic world-class city cluster and an internationally influential
    center for scientific and technological innovation；be developed into a high-quality
    living circle suitable for working, shopping and tourism。
    中文没有"不仅……还"就直接跳过这一句，别硬套。

[5] ［数据句：中文出现数字时必写，独立成句最清楚］
    It ［consists of 三条线路 / covers 里程 / implements 项目数］, with a total
    length of ［4350］ kilometers and benefiting over ［4亿］ people.

    对应中文："分为东、中、西三条线路，总长4350公里，惠及人口将超过4亿"或
    "拥有110多立方米使用空间……在距地球表面400-450公里的轨道上运行"。
    空里填：阿拉伯数字照抄；亿 = 100 million（4亿 = 400 million）；
    "110多立方米" = over 110 cubic meters；"400-450公里" = 400-450 kilometers；
    "180多个项目" = over 180 projects；"数千亿元" = hundreds of billions of yuan。
    数字+单位是送分点，写错单位扣分比少写一个形容词狠得多。

[6] ［应用句，同中版[4]；若[5]已经写了数据，这里可换成"惠及对象"］
    ［成果］ have made ［优质资源］ accessible to ［更多人群］.

    对应中文："远程医疗使优质医疗资源惠及更多患者"。
    空里填：telemedicine has made quality medical resources accessible to more patients。
    "使……可获得/惠及"固定用 make ... accessible to，别用 benefit 硬翻。

[7] ［结果句，同中版[5]］.

[8] ［收尾句，同中版[6]，或用 making 结果状语收尾］
    ［主体］ is ［injecting unprecedented vitality into / better meeting the needs of］
    ［领域］, making ［中国/社会］ ［受益结果］.

    对应中文："正在为中国农村发展注入前所未有的活力""更好地满足了人民的健康需求"。
    空里填：injecting unprecedented vitality into China's rural development；
    better meet the health needs of the people；deep integration of technology and medicine
    （"深度融合"固定说 the deep integration of A and B）。
```

长版不是让你每段都写满8句，而是给你**8个可插拔的模块**：哪句中文对应得上，就把哪句抽出来用。阅卷看的是"该有的句子有没有"，不是"句子多不多"。

### 1.5 真题验证：把3段真题中文套进骨架

下面三段全部用**中版骨架**现场填。左边是骨架空位，右边是填完的整句，一眼能看出"空对应中文的哪块"。

#### 示范一：洋山港（2024.12-2）——套 定位句+背景句+过程句+应用省略+结果句+收尾句

中文原文：洋山港（Yangshan Port）是上海航运中心的重要组成部分，是中国第一个深水港，也是世界上规模最大的深水港之一。经过近20年的发展，洋山港已实现高度自动化。数字技术和人工智能的使用大大减少了用工成本和碳排放。自主研发的码头管理系统可以在百公里之外对大型设备进行远程操控。洋山港看上去一片繁忙，现场却见不到人工操作，而且能够24小时不间断运作。洋山港将不断发展，为把上海建成一个全球航运中心做出更大贡献。

逐句套骨架：

```
[1] 定位句B → Yangshan Port is an important component of Shanghai's shipping center,
    China's first deep-water port, and one of the largest deep-water ports in the world.
    （三个"身份"并列：一个 of 短语 + 同位语 + one of the largest；原样背下来，换名词就能用。）

[2] 背景句 → After nearly 20 years of development, Yangshan Port has achieved
    a high degree of automation.
    （"经过近20年的发展" = After nearly 20 years of development；"高度自动化" = a high degree of automation。）

[3] 过程句 → The use of digital technology and artificial intelligence has significantly
    reduced labor costs and carbon emissions. The self-developed terminal management
    system enables remote control of large equipment from over a hundred kilometers away.
    （中文两句都讲"怎么做到的"，合成两句并列；"百公里之外" = from over a hundred kilometers away。）

[5] 结果句 → Yangshan Port appears bustling, yet no manual operations are visible
    on site, and it operates 24 hours a day without interruption.
    （"看上去繁忙却见不到人工"是转折，用 yet；"24小时不间断" = operates 24 hours a day without interruption。）

[6] 收尾句 → Yangshan Port will continue to develop and make greater contributions
    to building Shanghai into a global shipping center.
    （"做出更大贡献" = make greater contributions to building ... into ...。）
```

填好的完整译文（和官方参考译文逐句一致，只是把段落连排）：

> Yangshan Port is an important component of Shanghai's shipping center, China's first deep-water port, and one of the largest deep-water ports in the world. After nearly 20 years of development, Yangshan Port has achieved a high degree of automation. The use of digital technology and artificial intelligence has significantly reduced labor costs and carbon emissions. The self-developed terminal management system enables remote control of large equipment from over a hundred kilometers away. Yangshan Port appears bustling, yet no manual operations are visible on site, and it operates 24 hours a day without interruption. Yangshan Port will continue to develop and make greater contributions to building Shanghai into a global shipping center.

#### 示范二：无人机（2026.06-3）——套 定位句C+背景句+应用句+数据句省略+过程句+应用句+收尾句

中文原文：近年来，中国无人机（drone）产业蓬勃发展，出口量跃升为全球第一。中国的无人机以出色的稳定性、高清（high-definition）拍摄能力和便捷的操作，受到全球消费者的喜爱，在民用领域得到广泛应用。无人机已被国家纳入战略性新兴产业，逐步成为能满足多种场景需求的智能助手。在农村，无人机可精准识别农作物病虫害，高效喷洒农药。在城市，无人机可快速运送生鲜食品。在不久的将来，无人机能够在更多领域实现其独特价值，为经济社会发展注入新动能。

逐句套骨架：

```
[1] 定位句C → In recent years, China's drone industry has witnessed remarkable growth,
    with its export volume leaping to the top worldwide.
    （"蓬勃发展" = has witnessed remarkable growth；"跃升为全球第一" = with its export
    volume leaping to the top worldwide——又是一个 with 伴随结构。）

[4] 应用句 → Chinese drones are favored by global consumers for their excellent
    stability, high-definition imaging capabilities, and user-friendly operation, and
    have been widely applied in the civilian sector.
    （"以……受到喜爱" = are favored ... for ...；三个优点并列；"受到广泛应用" = have been widely applied。）

[2]+[3] 背景/过程 → Drones have been included in the national strategic emerging
    industries and are gradually becoming intelligent assistants capable of meeting
    diverse needs.
    （"被纳入" = have been included in——被动；"能满足多种场景需求" = capable of meeting diverse needs。）

[6] 应用句变体（分场景列举）→ In rural areas, drones can accurately identify crop
    pests and diseases and efficiently spray pesticides. In cities, they can rapidly
    deliver fresh food.
    （中文"在农村……在城市……"是典型分场景列举，直接照搬 In rural areas / In cities 两个开头。）

[8] 收尾句 → In the near future, drones will be able to realize their unique value
    in more fields, injecting new momentum into economic and social development.
    ("注入新动能" = injecting new momentum into，现在分词收尾。)
```

填好的完整译文（与官方参考译文一致）：

> In recent years, China's drone industry has witnessed remarkable growth, with its export volume leaping to the top worldwide. Chinese drones are favored by global consumers for their excellent stability, high-definition imaging capabilities, and user-friendly operation, and have been widely applied in the civilian sector. Drones have been included in the national strategic emerging industries and are gradually becoming intelligent assistants capable of meeting diverse needs. In rural areas, drones can accurately identify crop pests and diseases and efficiently spray pesticides. In cities, they can rapidly deliver fresh food. In the near future, drones will be able to realize their unique value in more fields, injecting new momentum into economic and social development.

#### 示范三：远程医疗（2026.06-1）——套 定位句+背景句+过程句×3+应用句+收尾句

中文原文：科技进步使中国的医疗事业得到快速发展。近年来，远程医疗使优质医疗资源惠及更多患者。医学专家可以通过视频为偏远地区患者提供咨询，指导当地医生制定并实施治疗方案。人工智能辅助诊断系统帮助医生识别患者的早期症状，提高诊断准确率。电子健康档案的普及让医生能够快速了解患者病史，避免重复检查。线上医疗咨询平台极大地方便了患者就诊。科技与医疗的深度融合更好地满足了人民的健康需求。

逐句套骨架：

```
[1] 定位句 → Technological advances have led to rapid development in China's
    healthcare sector.
    （"科技进步使……得到快速发展" = Technological advances have led to rapid development in ...。）

[2] 背景句 → In recent years, telemedicine has made quality medical resources
    accessible to more patients.
    （"使……惠及更多患者" = has made ... accessible to more patients。）

[3] 过程句（这段有三句并列的"帮助/让"，全用同一个骨架）→
    Medical experts can provide consultations via video to patients in remote areas
    and guide local doctors in formulating and implementing treatment plans.
    AI-assisted diagnostic systems help doctors identify early symptoms in patients
    and improve diagnostic accuracy.
    The widespread use of electronic health records enables doctors to quickly
    understand patients' medical histories and avoid redundant examinations.
    （"制定并实施" = formulating and implementing；"早期症状" = early symptoms；
    "避免重复检查" = avoid redundant examinations。三个动词 lead to / make / help / enable
    换着用，避免重复。）

[6] 应用句 → Online medical consultation platforms have greatly facilitated
    patient access to healthcare.
    （"极大地方便了患者就诊" = have greatly facilitated patient access to healthcare。）

[8] 收尾句 → The deep integration of technology and medicine better meets the
    health needs of the people.
    （"深度融合" = the deep integration of A and B；"更好地满足了……需求" = better meets the needs of ...。）
```

填好的完整译文（与官方参考译文一致）：

> Technological advances have led to rapid development in China's healthcare sector. In recent years, telemedicine has made quality medical resources accessible to more patients. Medical experts can provide consultations via video to patients in remote areas and guide local doctors in formulating and implementing treatment plans. AI-assisted diagnostic systems help doctors identify early symptoms in patients and improve diagnostic accuracy. The widespread use of electronic health records enables doctors to quickly understand patients' medical histories and avoid redundant examinations. Online medical consultation platforms have greatly facilitated patient access to healthcare. The deep integration of technology and medicine better meets the health needs of the people.

## 第二部分　美德类：3段倒推出来的成品骨架

### 2.1 美德类的骨架和成就类不一样

尊老、节俭、自强自立这三段，开头都是"……是中华民族的传统美德"，中间讲"古/今对比 + 政府/社会怎么做"，结尾都是"有助于……/成为……的强大动力"。它有一条成就类没有的固定套路：**先定性（传统美德）→ 再扎根（deeply rooted in）→ 古今对照 → 全社会行动 → 收尾升华**。

### 2.2 美德类成品骨架（5句，约110-125词）

```
[1] ［美德名称］ is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation, deeply rooted
    in ［文化/思想行为］, and it serves as ［行为规范/重要支撑］.

    对应中文："……是中华民族的传统美德，深深植根于……，是……的行为规范/重要支撑"。
    空里填：尊老 = Respecting the elderly；节俭 = Frugality；自强自立 = the spirit of
    self-reliance and independence；深深植根于 = deeply rooted in the thoughts and
    behaviors of Chinese people / in Chinese culture；
    行为规范和社会准则 = a code of conduct and social norm widely observed；
    民族生存与发展的重要支撑 = a vital pillar for the nation's survival and development。
    （这段开头三句几乎是三段共用的，整句背下来，换美德名字就能用。）

[2] In ancient China, when ［生产力/条件］ was ［低下］, our ancestors
    ［深知……来之不易 / 秉持……理念］.

    对应中文：节俭那段独有的"在中国古代，生产力低下，先人们深知劳动成果来之不易，因而秉持节俭的理念"。
    空里填：productivity was low；were well aware that the fruits of labor were
    hard-won（"来之不易" = hard-won，固定译法）；upheld the principle of thrift。
    尊老和自强自立没有古今对比，这一句直接删掉，不硬套。

[3] Even in today's world of ［物质丰富/竞争环境］, the Chinese people still
    ［坚持……生活方式 / 崇尚……精神］, reflecting ［理性消费的理念 / 对劳动的尊重］.

    对应中文："到了物质极大丰富的今天，中国人民依然坚持节俭的生活方式，体现了理性消费的生活理念和对劳动的尊重"。
    空里填：today's world of material abundance；adhere to a frugal lifestyle；
    a philosophy of rational consumption and respect for labor。
    如果没有"今昔对比"，这句换成："In today's China, this virtue has been widely
    inherited."（当今中国，这种美德得到广泛传承——尊老原文就是这句。）

[4] In recent years, the Chinese government/the whole society has continuously
    ［加强建设/营造氛围］, advocating ［举措1］, reducing ［问题］, and promoting ［举措2］.

    对应中文：政府/社会各界的具体动作，用三个并列动名词收尾。
    空里填：intensified efforts to build a conservation-minded society（建设节约型社会）；
    advocating the "Clean Plate Campaign"（倡导"光盘行动"）；reducing food waste；
    promoting a simple and low-carbon lifestyle；advancing the recycling of resources；
    opposing excessive consumption。
    尊老那段的社会动作写成：All sectors of society are actively fostering a social
    atmosphere of respect and care for the elderly. It has become a social consensus
    to provide convenient services for the elderly, such as setting up special dining
    halls for seniors and offering priority seating in public places.
    （"长者食堂" = dining halls for seniors；"优先座位" = priority seating。）

[5] ［美德名称］ is a ［crucial cornerstone/powerful driving force］ of
    ［社会和谐与发展/现代化进程］, helping to ［培育氛围/推动进步］ and
    ［激励人民……］.

    对应中文："是……的重要基石/强大动力，有助于……，推动……"或"激励着中国人民为实现……不断奋进"。
    空里填：a crucial cornerstone of social harmony and development（重要基石）；
    a powerful driving force behind the country's modernization（强大动力）；
    helping to cultivate a favorable social atmosphere and promote the progress of
    civilization throughout society；inspiring the Chinese people to strive
    relentlessly for the Chinese Dream of national rejuvenation。
    （"激励……不断奋进" = inspiring ... to strive relentlessly for ...。）
```

### 2.3 真题验证：3段美德全填一遍

#### 尊老（2025.12-1）

填好的完整译文（与官方参考译文一致）：

> Respecting the elderly is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation, deeply rooted in the thoughts and behaviors of Chinese people, and it serves as a code of conduct and social norm widely observed. In today's China, this virtue has been widely inherited. All sectors of society are actively fostering a social atmosphere of respect and care for the elderly. It has become a social consensus to provide convenient services for the elderly, such as setting up special dining halls for seniors in communities and offering priority seating in public places. The government has also introduced a series of policies to provide strong protection for the rights and interests of the elderly. Respecting the elderly is a crucial cornerstone of social harmony and development, helping to cultivate a favorable social atmosphere and promote the progress of civilization throughout society.

骨架对应：[1] 定性句 → [3] 当今传承 → [4] 社会共识（长者食堂/优先座位）→ [4] 政府政策 → [5] 重要基石收尾。没有古今对比，[2] 删掉。

#### 节俭（2025.12-3）

填好的完整译文（与官方参考译文一致）：

> Frugality is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation. In ancient China, when productivity was low, our ancestors were well aware that the fruits of labor were hard-won, and thus they upheld the principle of thrift. Even in today's world of material abundance, the Chinese people still adhere to a frugal lifestyle, reflecting a philosophy of rational consumption and respect for labor. In recent years, the Chinese government has continuously intensified efforts to build a conservation-minded society, advocating the "Clean Plate Campaign" to reduce food waste, promoting a simple and low-carbon lifestyle, advancing the recycling of resources, and opposing excessive consumption. The construction of a conservation-oriented society has effectively propelled the high-quality development of China's economy.

骨架对应：[1] 定性 → [2] 古代生产力低 → [3] 今天物质丰富 → [4] 政府四项举措（并列动名词）→ [5] 高质量发展收尾。

#### 自强自立（2025.12-2）

填好的完整译文（与官方参考译文一致）：

> The Chinese nation champions the spirit of self-reliance and independence, which is deeply rooted in Chinese culture and serves as a vital pillar for the nation's survival and development. Since the reform and opening-up, China has continuously increased investment in scientific research on its journey of self-reliance, achieving new breakthroughs in fields such as information technology, artificial intelligence, and aerospace engineering. The nationwide coverage of 5G networks, the rapid development of telemedicine, the global leadership in the use of electronic payments, and the Chang'e lunar exploration program all demonstrate remarkable progress in the aerospace sector. The spirit of self-reliance has become a powerful driving force behind the country's modernization, inspiring the Chinese people to strive relentlessly for the Chinese Dream of national rejuvenation.

骨架对应：[1] 定性（注意这里用 champions 替换 is a traditional virtue，且用 which 引导非限定性定语从句）→ [3] 科研投入 + achieving 结果状语 → [4] 四个并列成果 → [5] 强大动力 + inspiring 收尾。

**三段放一起看，[1] 和 [5] 是共用的，中间 [2][3][4] 按题目挑。这就是"能背能默写只换关键词"的美德篇。**

## 第三部分　成品级"怎么拆中文"指令

拿到一段中文，按下面五步走，每步都配真题例子。这一步是骨架能填对的前提——**先拆对，再填词**。

### 第0步：数逗号，把中文切成"小句"

中文一个逗号就是一处可断可连的地方。先拿笔把逗号圈出来，数清这段有几小句。六级这段一般是6-8小句，对应译文4-7句。

例（洋山港）：
> 洋山港是上海航运中心的重要组成部分，①是中国第一个深水港，②也是世界上规模最大的深水港之一。③经过近20年的发展，④洋山港已实现高度自动化。

拆完：①三个"身份"小句 → 合成英语一句（用并列）；②时间状语小句 → 放句首；③主谓小句 → 主干。

### 第1步：找主语——先把"……的"拎出来

中文爱用"……的"开头做长定语，英语可以把整个"……的"拎出来当主语。

例：
> "北斗卫星导航系统的成功研制是……一项重大科技成就"
> 原中文看着是"系统"做主语，其实真正的主语是"成功研制"这件事。
> 拆完：`［成功研制］ of the Beidou system` → 译：The successful development of the Beidou satellite navigation system is a major technological achievement...

规则：**"X的成功研制/蓬勃发展/深度融合" → "The successful development / booming development / deep integration of X"**，直接当句子主语，后面接 is/has led to。

### 第2步：长句怎么切——三小句以上拆两句

中文一句里塞了4-5个小句（逗号多）时，英语一句装不下，按"语义层次"切成两句。南水北调那段是教科书例子。

例（南水北调）：
> 原中文：历经数十年的规划与筹备，工程于2002年开工建设，分为东、中、西三条线路，总长4350公里，惠及人口将超过4亿。

拆完（两层）：
- 第一层（筹划+开工+惠及人口）→ 一句：After decades of planning and preparation, the project, consisting of three routes with a total length of 4,350 kilometers, commenced construction in 2002 and is expected to benefit over 400 million people.
- 第二层（线路属性）→ 用破折号/with 塞进上一句，不再单独成句。

规则：**"历经……，于……开工，分为……，总长……，惠及……"** 这种5连小句，主干只留"工程于2002年开工建设"一个谓语，其余全变成 with 结构、后置定语、插入语。

### 第3步：被动怎么判断——看主语是"做事的"还是"被弄的"

中文"被/受到/得到/已纳入/得到广泛应用"是明被动；还有一类中文没"被"字但主语是承受者（如"这一精神深植于……"），英语也要用被动或系表。

例（无人机）：
> 原中文：无人机已被国家纳入战略性新兴产业，逐步成为……智能助手。
> 判断："无人机"不能自己"纳入"，是被纳入 → 译：Drones have been included in the national strategic emerging industries...
> 同段："在民用领域得到广泛应用" → 无人机是被应用 → have been widely applied in the civilian sector。

例（尊老）：
> "这种美德得到广泛传承" → 美德是被传承 → this virtue has been widely inherited。

规则：**看到"得到/受到/已被/得以 + 动词"，一律 be + 过去分词**；主语是物/抽象概念且不可能自己发出动作时，也用被动。

### 第4步：数字怎么转——照抄+换算两件事

例（南水北调/天宫）：
- 阿拉伯数字照抄：2003年 → in 2003；2022年 → in 2022；4350公里 → 4,350 kilometers。
- 亿 = 100 million：4亿 → 400 million；500亿立方米 → 50 billion cubic meters；数千亿元 → hundreds of billions of yuan。
- 范围用连字符：400-450公里 → 400-450 kilometers。
- "多/余" = over：110多立方米 → over 110 cubic meters；180多个项目 → over 180 projects。

规则：**数字和单位绑在一起写，宁可删修饰语也别漏数字**。数字句是8/12段中文的必考点。

### 第5步：专名怎么办——括号照抄，没给就"拼音+类别词"

例：
- 括号给了英文的直接抄：Beidou、Yangshan Port、Tiangong Space Station、the South-to-North Water Diversion Project、drone、high-definition、live-streaming commerce、frugality、rejuvenation。
- 没给英文的用"拼音/意译 + 上位类别词"：嫦娥探月工程 → the Chang'e lunar exploration program；光盘行动 → the "Clean Plate Campaign"；太空育种 → space breeding。
- 美德小词库：virtue（美德）、frugality（节俭）、respect for the elderly（尊老）、self-reliance（自强自立）、harmony（和谐）、well-being（福祉）、code of conduct（行为规范）。

规则：**括号里现成的英文一个字母都别改**；没给的先拼音，再补一个 category 词让人看懂。

## 最后：考场上怎么在30分钟内用出来

1. 先花2分钟把中文按第0步数逗号、切小句，标出哪句对应骨架哪一位（定位/背景/过程/应用/数据/结果/收尾）。
2. 草稿纸默写出骨架的对应句子（短版4句或中版6句），先写骨架再往里填词。
3. 按第五步处理专名（括号照抄）、按第四步处理数字（别漏单位）、按第三步处理被动（得到/受到 → be done）。
4. 成就类结尾优先用 making 结果状语，美德类结尾优先用 helping/inspiring 现在分词——这两招参考译文用了不下十次，稳。
5. 字数落在105-139词、4-7句就与官方译文体量一致，不要贪多写复杂句；写对简单句比写错复杂句得分高。

把第一部分三个骨架版本和第二部分美德骨架整段背下来，再把第四部分六个位置的素材包各记3-5句，考场上就是"看到中文→拆成小句→选骨架→填空→收尾"，和作文模板一样是肌肉记忆。
